pawbs: a fast, private e621 & e926 viewer

pawbs is a free e621 client that runs in your browser. Search, browse and save posts from e621 and e926 in a fast, clean interface, with no ads, no tracking and nothing to install from an app store. e621 is a furry art archive for adults; e926 is its safe-for-work version.

What you can do

  • Search tags with autocomplete (aliases, post counts, categories) and a query assistant that writes e621's search syntax for you
  • Save searches, keep your history, and see the tags trending and rising today
  • Follow tags and see how many new posts each one got since you last looked
  • Read pools in order, browse sets, and see popular posts by day, week or month
  • Keep favourites and collections, plus your e621 favourites when you sign in
  • Find similar pictures, and an artist's other galleries and links
  • View posts with zoom, slideshow, video controls with an A-B loop, keyboard shortcuts and touch gestures
  • Hide what you never want to see with a blacklist (yours is imported from your e621 account)
  • Download posts one by one or many at once, such as a whole pool, as a link list for your download manager
  • Move everything to another device with an encrypted transfer, or keep a backup file

All pawbs features, and more on e621 search, the pool reader and downloads.

On every device

pawbs is a progressive web app (PWA), made for mobile as much as for desktop: open pawbs.app in any modern browser. Add it to your home screen on iPhone, iPad or Android, or install it on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS: it opens in its own window like an app, and starts even when you are offline. Questions

Is pawbs affiliated with e621?

No. pawbs is an independent, unofficial client. It talks to e621 and e926 through their public API.

Do I need an e621 account?

No, you can browse as a guest. To see your favourites and sets, sign in with your e621 user name and an API key from your e621 account settings, never your password.

Where does my data go?

Your searches, favourites, follows and settings stay in your browser. Requests go straight from your browser to e621 or e926, and your API key is only ever sent to them. pawbs has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Details on the privacy page.

How do I install pawbs on an iPhone?

Open pawbs.app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, choose Install app in the browser menu; on a computer, use the install button in the address bar of Chrome or Edge.

What is e926?

e926 is the safe-for-work version of e621. pawbs works with both: pick the one you want when you set it up. See pawbs for e926.

Is pawbs free?

Yes, pawbs is free to use.
